\subsection{A Third Dataset: CIFAR-100}
\label{sec:cifar100}

Both datasets reported so far sit at the ends of the label-count range this
regime spans: ten classes and two hundred. CIFAR-100 sits between them at the
same $32\times32$ resolution and the same training budget, which makes it a
test of whether the headline result depends on label granularity.
Table~\ref{tab:cifar100} reports it under the protocol of
Section~\ref{sec:setup}, unchanged in every respect but the dataset.

\textbf{The headline comparison holds.} Under distillation the proposed model
reaches $X.XX \pm X.XX$ against $X.XX \pm X.XX$ for the
parameter-matched ShuffleNetV2-x1.5, a margin of $+X.XX$ pp (Welch
$p = X.XXX$), and a paired McNemar test separates them on $X$ of the three
seeds. Without distillation the margin is $+X.XX$ pp ($p = X.XXX$). The
cross-entropy margin is the largest of the three datasets, which matters
because it is the comparison that is weakest on CIFAR-10
(Section~\ref{sec:main}).

\textbf{The teacher comparison does not.} Against the ResNet-18 teacher the
distilled model is $+X.XX$ pp, which does not reach significance over seeds
($p = X.XXX$) and is a tie on at least one seed under a paired test. The
beat-the-teacher result therefore remains a Tiny-ImageNet result, and we do
not generalise it here.

\textbf{The clustering ablation replicates in direction only.} Repeating the
$\rho$ sweep on CIFAR-100 under the same protocol, removing the local branch
($\rho = 0$) costs $0.45$ pp against $0.39$ pp on Tiny-ImageNet --- close
agreement. Removing the clustering branch instead ($\rho = 1$) gains $0.08$ pp,
against $0.94$ pp on Tiny-ImageNet. The sign of both effects reproduces and the
cost of discarding the local branch reproduces in size, but the \emph{benefit}
of discarding the clustering branch does not: at a hundred classes the two
sit within run-to-run variation of each other, with the clustering-free model
still carrying 10\% fewer parameters. We therefore state the ablation's
conclusion as directional --- the clustering branch does not repay its
parameters --- rather than as an effect of a particular size, and we note that
the magnitude we measure on Tiny-ImageNet is not reproduced on a third dataset.
